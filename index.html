PRODUCT THINKING

Why this prototype?

Fantasy managers rarely lack information. The challenge is turning performance, usage, matchup, coaching context and player availability into a decision they can trust. This MVP combines nine explainable signals into a transparent comparison model instead of hiding the recommendation behind a black box.

01 Explainable

Nine weighted signals drive each recommendation, with an experimental trench matchup displayed separately at 0% model weight.

02 Data-driven

Real NFL performance, usage, matchup and coaching-context data power the model.

03 Iterative

The model is designed to be tested against real outcomes and improved over time.
